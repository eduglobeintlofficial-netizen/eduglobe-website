About EduGlobe

Helping students navigate global education.

EduGlobe International is a Bangladesh-based, online education guidance initiative supporting students who want to explore higher-education opportunities overseas.

We aim to make the process easier to understand—from researching suitable courses and universities to preparing applications and learning about scholarship and student-visa requirements.

Our approach

Student-centred. Guidance shaped around your academic background and goals.

Transparent. Clear information about processes, requirements and service fees.

Responsible. No promises of guaranteed admission, scholarships or visa approval.
